Given that both A and B are n<ruby>×<rt>by</rt></ruby>n matrices satisfying A+B=AB.
Prove that A-E is invertible.

Proof:<br>
A+B=AB<br>
A=AB-B<br>
A=(A-E)B<br>
A<strong>-(A-E)</strong>=(A-E)B<strong>-(A-E)</strong><br>
E=(A-E)(B-E)<br>
∴A-E is invertible.<br>
<ruby>Q.E.D.<rt>Quod Erat Demonstrandum</rt></ruby><br>
(*For square matrices of the same order, if XY=E, then X is invertible, and X<sup>-1</sup>=Y.*)
